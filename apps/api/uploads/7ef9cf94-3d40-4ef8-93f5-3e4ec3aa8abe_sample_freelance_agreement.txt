# FREELANCE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

**Document Type:** Freelance Service Agreement  
**Agreement Date:** 1 November 2026

## 1. Parties

**Client:** ABC Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Freelancer:** John Smith  
**Project:** Business Website Development

## 2. Project Scope

The freelancer agrees to design and develop a business website containing:
- Home page
- About Us page
- Services page
- Contact page
- Responsive mobile design

Any additional features will require separate approval and payment.

## 3. Project Payment

The total project cost is **₹80,000**.

The payment schedule is:
- 40% advance payment: ₹32,000 before project commencement.
- 40% milestone payment: ₹32,000 after completion of the website design.
- 20% final payment: ₹16,000 after final delivery.

## 4. Payment Due Date

The client must pay each invoice within **15 days** of receiving it.

## 5. Late Payment Penalty

If payment is delayed beyond the due date, the freelancer may charge a late payment fee of 2% per month on the outstanding amount.

## 6. Project Deadline

The freelancer agrees to complete the project by **30 November 2026**.

Delays caused by the client, including late content delivery or delayed approvals, may extend the project deadline.

## 7. Client Responsibilities

The client must:
- Provide website content, images, and branding materials.
- Review project milestones.
- Provide feedback within five working days.

## 8. Freelancer Responsibilities

The freelancer must:
- Develop the website according to the agreed scope.
- Provide regular project updates.
- Fix bugs identified within 15 days after delivery.

## 9. Project Changes

Any changes outside the agreed scope may involve additional charges and revised deadlines. The freelancer must obtain client approval before beginning additional work.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing seven days' written notice.

The client must pay for all completed work and approved expenses up to the termination date.

## 11. Dispute Resolution

Both parties agree to attempt to resolve disputes through mutual discussion before pursuing other remedies.

## 12. Acceptance

Both parties acknowledge that they have read and agreed to the terms of this agreement.
